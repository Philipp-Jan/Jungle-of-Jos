About Jos

Jos has been guiding trekkers through the jungle of Bukit Lawang for many years. Born and raised right here, he's the youngest of eight siblings, and his whole family calls this village home — this jungle isn't just his workplace, it's where he grew up. He leads treks himself, backed by a small team of local guides who know these trails just as well as he does. Jos speaks English and is always happy to share stories about the forest, its animals, and village life along the way. He's also a proud father of two, and when he's not out trekking, he's happy to help arrange onward travel too — whether that's a bus or private car to Medan, Berastagi, or Lake Toba.
