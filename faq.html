Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your jungle trek

PLACEHOLDER: Explain the booking process step by step — there is no online booking system, everything happens via WhatsApp. Cover: how to message Jos, what info to include (arrival date in Bukit Lawang, preferred start date/tour), how meeting point and time are confirmed, whether transfer from the Bukit Lawang bus station or hotel is needed/available, and that Jos can also help organize transport to/from Bukit Lawang (e.g. Medan, Berastagi, Lake Toba).

PLACEHOLDER: Payment is cash only, paid in person — no online payment or deposit required. Mention this is based on trust since there's no advance booking system.

Also mention what can be left at the hotel (e.g. large luggage, valuables not needed on trek).

PLACEHOLDER: Fitness requirements — general trekking fitness level needed, note if any tour requires more stamina than others.
