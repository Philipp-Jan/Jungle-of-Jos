Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your jungle trek

The process to book a tour is very easy. Just get in touch with Jos via WhatsApp - there is no online booking system. Cover: how to If you already know it, you can include:

  • Arrival date in Bukit Lawang
  • Preferred start date and which tour you would like to book
  • If you need transfer from Bukit Lawang bus station
  • Number of clients

Group size: All tours require a minimum of 2 people to run. If you're traveling alone, Jos can often place you with an existing group — but this can't always be guaranteed for the next available day, so you may need to join a group whose trek is already scheduled. Let Jos know as early as possible if you're booking solo.

Payment is cash only, paid in person — no online payment or deposit required. Please bring the exact amount in cash. You can withdraw cash in some hotels as well, but they will cahrge a fee. It is recommended to get it already in advance.

The packing list depends heavily on the tour you want to book. General, for all tours, bring:

  • Light backpack
  • Rain coat
  • Boots for hiking (for shorter treks sneakers are enough, for longer treks, we recommend hiking shoes) ∧ ne pair of Flip-Flops or similar for the evenings
  • Insect repellent & optional sunscreen
  • One big waterbottle (should last you for the trekking part. can be refilled only at the camps)
  • Long pants and long socks for hiking.
  • Swimwear & towel
  • Enough other clothes for the hiking days. Clotes can be washed in the river, but drying depends on weather conditions

You can leave the rest of your luggage at the hotel during your trekking. Just bring required equipment. Consider weather conditions yourself. If the forecast predicts rain, prepare some extra cloths.

In the multi day treks, most of the days you will be walking through the jungle for several hours. There are no mountains to be climbed but terrain can be difficult and there is some altitude to be overcome. A basic fitness level is definately required. But Jos will adjust the tempo and difficulty to the needs of the group. For the shorter treks No specific fitness level is required.

In case of severe storms, a tour may be spontaneously postponed or cancelled for safety reasons, though this is rare. Since there's no advance payment, there's no refund process needed — this is handled on a trust basis between traveler and guide. Just prepare your packing according to the weather forecast.
