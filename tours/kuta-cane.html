7-Day Expedition to Kuta Cane

A challenging one-way trek deep through remote rainforest — for experienced adventurers

Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Price €700 per person
Route One-way (ends in Kuta Cane)

This is a different kind of trip from our other jungle treks — instead of a loop back to Bukit Lawang, this expedition trek crosses the rainforest one-way, ending in the town of Kuta Cane on the other side of the mountains. PLACEHOLDER: Expand with more detail — this route passes through remote lowland rainforest with a real chance of encountering wild elephants alongside orangutans, and covers terrain far more remote than our standard tours: no roads, no settlements, days of genuine wilderness. This is a serious, physically demanding trek recommended only for experienced, fit trekkers who want a true wilderness expedition rather than a typical jungle tour.
