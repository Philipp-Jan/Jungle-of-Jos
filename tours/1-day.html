1-Day Jungle Trek

A perfect introduction to the Bukit Lawang jungle

Duration 1 Day
Price €70 per person
Group return River tubing

This is the perfect choice if you're short on time but still want a real taste of the Sumatran jungle. You'll trek through the rainforest with Jos, keeping an eye out for long-tailed macaques, Thomas leaf monkeys, and — if you're lucky — wild orangutans swinging through the canopy. Along the way, Jos shares his knowledge of the forest and its animals, built up over a lifetime in Bukit Lawang. Around midday, you'll stop for a fresh jungle lunch and a snack of local fruit before continuing the trek. At the end of the day, instead of walking back, you'll float down the river on an inner tube — a relaxed and fun way to return to town. A great option for travelers who want to experience the jungle without committing to an overnight stay.
