1-Day Jungle Trek

A perfect introduction to the Bukit Lawang jungle

Duration 1 Day
Price €70 per person
Group return River tubing

This is the perfect choice if you're short on time but still want a real taste of the Sumatran jungle. You'll trek through the rainforest with Jos, keeping an eye out for long-tailed macaques, Thomas leaf monkeys, and — if you're lucky — wild orangutans swinging through the canopy. Along the way, Jos shares his knowledge of the forest and its animals, built up over a lifetime in Bukit Lawang. Around midday, you'll stop for a fresh jungle lunch and a snack of local fruit before continuing the trek. At the end of the day, instead of walking back, you'll float down the river on an inner tube — a relaxed and fun way to return to town. A great option for travelers who want to experience the jungle without committing to an overnight stay.

Itinerary

A rough outline of the day — actual timing may vary depending on wildlife sightings and group pace

  1. 9:00 AM

    Briefing & Start

    Jos will pick you up directly at your hotel. Make sure you prepared all your gear and are ready to go.

  2. 9:30 AM

    Trekking begins

    After all participants are gathered, you will enter the forest and make your way to the entrance of Gunung Leuser National Park.

  3. 11:00 AM

    Fruit Snack

    First quick break to enjoy the sounds of the jungle and a mix of tropical fruits.

  4. 1:00 PM

    Jungle Lunch

    Have lunch at a scenic spot in the jungle with some local dishes.

  5. 2:00 PM

    Slowly make your way back

    Strive through the jungle in the afternoon and head back to the starting point.

  6. 5:00 PM

    Tour Ends

    Tour ends back in the town. Jos will accompany you to the drop-off point at your hotel.
